# Community code of conduct

KannadaLLMBench is an open research and engineering project. Participants are expected to be respectful, technically constructive, and considerate of contributors with different linguistic backgrounds and levels of Kannada fluency.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory or demeaning language, publishing private information, deliberate benchmark/data poisoning, and hostile conduct intended to drive others away.

Disagreement about annotation, dialect, transliteration, methodology, or benchmark design is expected. Critique the evidence and proposal rather than the person. Kannada has regional and social variation; no contributor should present one speaker's usage as the only legitimate form without evidence.

Report serious conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer rather than escalating them in public threads. Maintainers may edit/remove content or restrict participation when needed to keep the project safe and productive.
